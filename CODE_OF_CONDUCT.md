# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

- [ ] We welcome contributors who help build CueRoom respectfully and safely.
- [ ] We do not tolerate harassment, threats, hate speech, or abuse.

## Expected Behavior

- [ ] Be direct and constructive.
- [ ] Respect privacy and security boundaries.
- [ ] Report vulnerabilities responsibly.
- [ ] Keep product discussions grounded in legal and safety constraints.

## Enforcement

- [ ] Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation for violations.
- [ ] Serious abuse can be reported through the security/contact channel listed in the repository.
